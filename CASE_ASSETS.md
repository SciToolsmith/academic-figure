# Case asset provenance

The preview images under `skills/sci-plot/assets/cases/` and the Python/R
source files under `skills/sci-plot/assets/case-packs/` are independent,
code-authored reconstructions created by the project owner for the study of
scientific-figure design decisions.

They are not:

- images copied from publisher PDFs or journal websites;
- source code supplied by the referenced papers or their authors;
- raw research datasets;
- claims of affiliation with the original authors, Nature, or Springer Nature.

The previews and source packs are included as design references. They teach
choices such as panel roles, uncertainty display, paired structure, evidence
ordering, and diagnostic layout. They are not automatically verified
production templates, and their numbers, labels, or scientific conclusions
must not be transplanted into new work.

Some previews retain dataset-scale labels or generic record identifiers so the
scientific-expression problem remains intelligible. These labels must not be
treated as input data, reanalysed, or presented as results generated by this
repository.

No paper-specific raw data are distributed in this release. Most source packs
therefore cannot reproduce their preview without user-supplied, independently
authorized inputs. `rf-0001` and `rf-0178` have explicitly synthetic smoke
input generators; those values are marked non-production and are never
scientific evidence.

The repository license governs the included project-authored previews and
source files unless an asset is explicitly marked otherwise in a future
release.
